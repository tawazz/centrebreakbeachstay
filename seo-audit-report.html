Small fixes, in order

Repair the phone link critical

OpenSEO found a broken internal link from /activities/ to /activities/+610899531896, which returned 404, a page-not-found result. The public footer contains <a href="+610899531896">(08) 9953 1896</a>.

Fix: In the WordPress footer, change the phone link to tel:+61899531896. Crawl the site again and confirm the old relative URL no longer appears.

Give the homepage a useful search title and summary warning

Direct HTML shows <title>Home - Centre Break Beach Stay</title>. OpenSEO recorded metaDescription: "". A meta description is the short summary that can appear under a page in search results.

Fix: In the WordPress or Yoast page editor, use the title Jurien Bay Accommodation Near Dynamite Bay | Centre Break Beach Stay and the summary Looking for Jurien Bay accommodation? Stay at Centre Break Beach Stay in Green Head, 15 minutes from Jurien Bay and 50 metres from Dynamite Bay.

Add the homepage main heading warning

OpenSEO reports missing-h1: 1 for the homepage. An H1 is the page's main heading, and the public homepage has no H1 in its HTML.

Fix: Add one visible H1 in the homepage hero, such as Centre Break Beach Stay near Jurien Bay. Keep the section headings below it as H2 headings, the next heading level.

Complete the activities page heading and summary warning

Direct HTML shows <title>Activities - Centre Break Beach Stay</title> with no meta description. The heading count is h1=1, h2=18, h3=0, h4=4, so the page jumps from H2 section headings to H4 subheadings without an H3 level. OpenSEO recorded one heading-order skip.

Fix: Set the title to Things to Do Near Jurien Bay & Green Head | Centre Break Beach Stay, add the summary Plan things to do near Jurien Bay and Green Head, including sea lions, beaches, snorkelling, fishing, the Three Bays Walkway, wildflowers and more., and change footer or sub-section labels to H3 or plain text so the heading levels descend in order.

Add image descriptions warning

OpenSEO reports imagesMissingAlt: 39 and imagesTotal: 42 on the homepage. Alt text is a short image description used by screen readers and shown when an image cannot load.

Fix: In the image or page-builder settings, describe meaningful hero, room, restaurant, and location images in a few words. Leave alt="" only on images that add decoration and no information.
